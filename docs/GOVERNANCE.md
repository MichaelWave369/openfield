# Governance

## Constitutional principles

1. Reality is the customer.
2. Truth survives best when its history survives with it.
3. Provenance is part of the finding.
4. Authority remains scoped, visible, and revocable.
5. Dissent is a protected system function.
6. Safety includes civil liberties and freedom from unjustified surveillance.

## Complementary chambers

**GovernOtter** checks scientific integrity: evidence, mathematics, dependencies, claim promotion, and custody.

**GovernOri** checks knowledge evolution: provenance, reconstructability, preserved dissent, scoped authority, and system health.

Neither chamber outranks the other. High-impact publication may require both plus a human operator.

OpenField must not be designed for stalking, doxxing, political repression, unlawful interception, discriminatory targeting, autonomous accusation, or removal of due process.
